Emergency Plumber Joliet IL Near Romeoville

Joliet is the largest city in our service area, and its houses range from 1850s limestone-era homes to recent subdivisions. We cover all of it with a 24/7 emergency line and a crew based just to the northeast.

Both sides of the river, all hours

Joliet is split east and west by the Des Plaines River, and a small part of the city lies in Kendall County. From Romeoville we reach the city to the southwest. Our emergency plumbers take calls 24/7 from downtown near Rialto Square Theatre to the neighborhoods around Joliet Junior College.

Route 66 runs through downtown. If you are calling from a commercial address, give the cross street, because many downtown blocks look alike on a navigation screen at night.

Water coming up through a floor drain in Joliet?

Stop using sinks, tubs and the washer. Do not run the dishwasher. Call the 24/7 emergency line and tell dispatch whether the water is clear or dirty. Dirty water means a sewer problem, and we plan the visit around that.

Houses that were built in the 1800s

The East Side Historic District has mostly homes built between 1852 and 1873. The Upper Bluff district west of the river holds houses from about 1850 to 1930. Many large homes were later split into apartments, which means extra kitchens and baths sharing old drain lines.

City well water, with Lake Michigan coming

Joliet's water comes from 21 deep bedrock wells and 5 shallow gravel wells. The city's 2024 water report says Lake Michigan water purchased from Chicago is expected at the tap in May 2030. The report does not list a hardness number, but it does say groundwater minerals cause hardness and the new supply should be softer.

If you own a softener, do not remove it now. Watch the city announcements before changing anything. Meanwhile, a water heater replacement is a good time to add a drain valve so the tank can be flushed.

Lead lines, main work and your service line

Joliet has replaced lead service lines since 2019. The city says it will replace a line at no charge to the homeowner after a leak repair disturbs it, when a test shows lead above the limit, or when it rehabilitates the main. Its lead inventory targets homes built before the 1940s.

The city is also replacing mains built before 1970, with about 30 miles planned in 2025 according to local news. Street work can stir up discolored water. Run the cold tap until it clears, and call if it does not. Before we repair a leaking service line, we point you to the city's lead page.

Storm sewers, lift stations and a flooded road

Public Utilities manages three wastewater plants, 598 miles of sanitary sewer, 556 miles of storm sewer and 50 lift stations. After heavy rain on August 1, 2026, news outlets reported several flooded roads, including County Line Road, Hammes Avenue and Republic Avenue. When roads flood, so do low basements and floor drains. Tell dispatch which road you can use.

Before we arrive in Joliet

Name the river side

East or west of the Des Plaines River. It decides which bridge the truck takes.

Mark the shutoff

Tape a note on the main valve or tell us where it is. In older houses it can sit behind stored boxes.

Skip the chemicals

Do not pour drain cleaner into a backed-up line. It stays in the pipe and burns whoever opens it.

Park clear

Leave room in front of the house or a driveway spot for the service truck.

Permits for plumbing in Joliet

The city enforces the current Illinois State Plumbing Code. Its inspections cover underground plumbing, sewer and water services, rough-in and final. Contractors must hold an Illinois plumbing contractor registration, carry insurance and file a letter of intent per project before a permit issues. We work under those rules for sewer line repair and other larger jobs.
