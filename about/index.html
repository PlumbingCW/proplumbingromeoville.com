About Romeoville Pro Plumbing

We are a plumbing company based on North Weber Road in Romeoville, Illinois. Our work is repairs, replacements and installs for homes and small businesses, and our line is open 24/7.

Call (630) 822-9290

Who we are

Romeoville Pro Plumbing is a licensed and insured plumbing company serving Romeoville and the towns around it. We handle burst pipes, clogged drains, water heaters, leaks, sewer lines, sump pumps and planned installs.

We keep the business simple. You call, a dispatcher listens, and a plumber is sent with the tools for the job you described. There is no sales form to fill out and no waiting for an email reply.

How a job runs

  1. You call the number on this page and describe what you see, hear or smell.
  2. Dispatch asks a few questions, gives you steps to limit damage and books the earliest same-day slot available.
  3. The plumber inspects the problem and explains the cause in plain words.
  4. You hear the price before any work starts. If the job changes, we tell you before continuing.
  5. The work is done, the area is cleaned up and we walk you through what was fixed.

Where we work

Our address is 402 N Weber Rd, Romeoville, IL 60446. Weber Road is the main commercial road through the village and connects to Interstate 55, so trucks leave from here for most of Romeoville in a short drive. For homes near the Des Plaines River or west toward Plainfield, the route is just as direct.

Beyond the village we cover Bolingbrook, Lockport, Plainfield, Joliet and Crest Hill. Our service area page lists the towns and their ZIP codes.

What we cover

The most common calls are for drain cleaning, water heater repair and leak detection. We also install fixtures, replace pipes and service sump pumps. Browse the full list of services for the rest.

How we handle pricing

Plumbing prices depend on the cause, the access and the parts. We do not guess over the phone. The plumber inspects the problem, tells you the price and waits for your yes before starting. After-hours rates, when they apply, are stated before the truck leaves.

What a call sounds like from our side

When you call the emergency line, the dispatcher asks four things. What is happening, where in the house, whether water or sewage is still moving, and whether anyone is in danger. If gas is involved, the answer is always the same: leave the building and call 911 and the gas utility first.

Those answers decide which truck goes out and what is on it. A basement floor drain that is backing up needs a different machine from a leaking water heater, and sending the right one the first time saves you a second visit.

Why we keep the work local

Houses in and around Romeoville range from 1950s homes to newer subdivisions, and the plumbing in them does not age the same way. Older homes may still have galvanized supply pipe or cast iron drains. Newer ones are more likely to have PEX and PVC. Knowing which you have changes the repair, so we ask about the age of the house on every call.

We also keep our service area tight. Bolingbrook, Lockport, Plainfield, Joliet and Crest Hill are all a short drive from Weber Road, which is why we list them by name instead of promising to go anywhere.

What to have ready before you call

  • Where the water is coming from and whether you can reach the shutoff valve
  • Which fixtures are affected and how long it has been happening
  • Roughly when the house was built and whether the pipes have been replaced
  • Whether you rent or own, because a landlord may need to approve the work

What we will not do

We will not pressure you into a bigger job than the problem needs. We will say when something is outside plumbing, such as a structural foundation crack or an electrical fault, and point you to the right trade. You can also ask any plumber for license details and verify them with the state before you hire.
