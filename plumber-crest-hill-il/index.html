Crest Hill lies south of Romeoville in Will County, inside Lockport Township. The city was incorporated in January 1960, and it has one ZIP code, 60403. Our emergency plumber service reaches it any hour of the day.

Is this a plumbing emergency or can it wait?

A dripping faucet, a noisy disposal or a stained but dry ceiling. Daytime appointments are plenty.

The city's rule on rainwater and sewers

Under city ordinance 15.21, no rain or groundwater may be piped into the sanitary sewer. Sump pumps, roof downspouts and yard drains are the usual culprits. The city also offers a cost-share: it pays half the bill for removing a clear-water hookup, capped at $4,000

That matters during a storm. Extra rainwater can swamp the sanitary lines and push sewage back up into basements. If your sump pump discharges into a floor drain, we can reroute it as part of a sump pump repair visit. Ask the city about the program before you start.

A well-fed system heading for Lake Michigan

Crest Hill uses its own water system, and news reports describe it as shallow wells. The city is one of six members of the Grand Prairie Water Commission, which will bring Lake Michigan water purchased from Chicago, with a target year of 2030. Romeoville and Joliet belong to the same commission.

We found no official hardness figure, so test your own tap rather than trust a number from a sales page. In the meantime, treat scale in your heater as likely. A water heater repair visit can include a look at the element, the anode rod and the drain valve.

After a main break or a hydrant flush

In October 2024, residents reported cloudy and green water, and city leaders attributed it to hydrant flushing. In November 2024, two water main breaks led to a boil order for homes south of Caton Farm Road. Events like these can leave sediment in your plumbing.

  1. Run the cold tap in the lowest sink until the water clears.
  2. Skip the washer and dishwasher until it does, since sediment can clog valve screens.
  3. Flush the water heater drain if hot water looks cloudy afterward.
  4. Call us if flow stays weak after the street has been cleared.

Neighborhoods, Lidice and older streets

Stern Park Gardens was later incorporated into Crest Hill and renamed Lidice in 1942, and the city keeps a Lidice Memorial on Hosmer Lane. Population grew from 5,887 in 1960 to 13,329 in 2000, so the housing stock mixes postwar construction with newer subdivisions. We did not find a verified age for any named neighborhood.

Permits and inspections

The City Building Department says most residential and commercial projects need a permit, and it follows the Illinois Plumbing Code. Plumbing inspections are available in the morning hours, from 8 a.m. to noon. New primary buildings need a water and wastewater permit application. Contractors must register with the city.
