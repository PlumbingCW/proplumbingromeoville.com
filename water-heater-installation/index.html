Water Heater Installation in Romeoville, IL

A planned water heater installation in Romeoville goes better than a rushed one. Pick the type, size and location with a licensed plumber, and the work is done once and done right. Our 24/7 line also takes scheduling calls.

Water heater installation in Romeoville, IL covers more than setting a tank on the floor. Venting, gas or electrical supply, a relief valve with a safe discharge pipe, and often an expansion tank all have to line up. We handle each piece and walk you through the choices before we start.

Start with the location

Where the heater sits decides much of the job. Most Romeoville homes keep it in a basement, a utility closet or a garage. A basement unit near the main drain is the easy case. Moving it to a new spot means new supply lines, a new vent path and sometimes a new drain.

A garage location in winter exposes the unit and its pipes to cold. Insulating the lines and keeping the tank clear of the door helps. A tight closet needs clearances to the walls that the manufacturer specifies, and we measure before we order.

Expansion tanks and why they matter

Water expands when it heats. In a closed system with a backflow device or pressure reducing valve on the supply, that extra volume has nowhere to go. Pressure climbs, the relief valve drips and fittings wear out early. A small expansion tank on the cold line absorbs the swing.

We test the household water pressure before we decide. If the pressure is already high, the tank needs a regulator as well. Ask us to explain the reading, and we show it to you on the gauge.

Code and venting items we check

  • Vent size, slope and clearance on gas units, with draft confirmed after lighting
  • A relief valve piped to a safe discharge point, not capped
  • A drain pan under units installed over finished floors
  • Shutoff valves on both the cold supply and the gas line
  • Dielectric or approved connectors where dissimilar metals meet

Permit rules vary, so ask the Village of Romeoville building department whether your job needs a permit. We install to the plumbing and gas codes that apply and tell you which parts of the job may involve an inspection.

Hard water and the tank you install

Mineral scale shortens the life of tank heaters and clogs tankless units. Ask the Village for its latest water quality report, or test your tap, before you pick a model. If the water is hard, a softener installed ahead of the heater protects the new equipment.
