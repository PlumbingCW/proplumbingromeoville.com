Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Romeoville, IL

The main sewer line carries every drain in your house to the street. When it clogs, the lowest fixture floods first, usually a basement floor drain or shower.

We clean main lines in Romeoville, IL 24/7, with machines sized for the pipe and a camera to confirm the result.

Emergency sewer line cleaning in Romeoville, IL is a 24/7 service for backups that reach floor drains, tubs and toilets. While you wait, stop all water use. Do not flush, run the washer or take a shower, because the line is full and every gallon pushes the backup higher.

Is it the main line or just one drain?

The easiest way to tell is to count. A single slow sink is a local clog. A main line problem affects the lowest and the most distant fixtures together. The pattern below helps you tell dispatch what is happening.

Backup right now: protect the house first

Sewage is not ordinary water

Keep children, pets and bare skin away from it. Avoid fans or devices plugged in near the wet area, and do not use a wet vacuum on raw sewage. Let a professional handle the cleanup after the line is open.

Basement floor drains in Romeoville homes sit at the lowest point of the plumbing. They are the first place a main line backup appears. If yours is about to overflow, move belongings off the floor and call.

How we clear a main line

Most main lines are cleared through a cleanout, a capped pipe near the foundation or in the basement. We attach a cutter head sized to the pipe to a drum machine and run it through the blockage. The head chews through paper, grease and soft roots and restores flow.

When grease or heavy scale lines the pipe, a hydro jetter does the second pass. A camera follows to confirm the line is open, so we are not just leaving a pinhole in the blockage.

What causes the line to clog

  • Roots entering at joints and building into a mat
  • Grease poured down kitchen drains that cools and coats the pipe
  • Wipes labeled flushable that do not break down
  • A bellied section where solids settle
  • A cracked or collapsed pipe that traps debris

Cleaning fixes the symptom. It does not fix a cracked or sagging pipe. If backups come back within months, the line probably has a defect, and the answer is sewer line repair rather than another cleaning.

Can't find your cleanout?

Where to look

Check the basement near the main stack, along the foundation wall closest to the street, and in the crawlspace or garage. A round or square cap, usually three or four inches across, marks it. If you cannot find one, say so when you call and we will work through a toilet.

After the cleaning: keep it from coming back

  1. Pour cooled cooking grease into a can for the trash, not down the sink.
  2. Flush only toilet paper and human waste.
  3. Consider a camera inspection if the line has clogged more than once.
  4. Keep trees and shrubs with aggressive roots away from the sewer path.
  5. Ask about annual cleaning if your line runs long and passes under mature trees.

Codes and permits

Cleaning a sewer line does not alter the pipe. If a camera look turns up damage that needs repair, that work follows the Illinois Plumbing Code and the Village of Romeoville building department sets the permit steps.
