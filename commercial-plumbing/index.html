Commercial plumbing is not residential plumbing at a larger scale. Pipes are bigger, fixtures see constant use, and one failure can close a restroom, a kitchen or a whole unit in a shared building. We are available 24/7 for urgent calls and also book planned work, so you can fix a recurring problem on a schedule that suits your hours.

If water is spreading through your building right now

  1. Find the main shutoff for the unit or building and close it. If you rent, ask the property manager where it is before an emergency, not during one.
  2. Cut power to the affected area from a dry spot, and call an electrician if the panel itself is wet.
  3. Move inventory, paper files and equipment off the floor, and photograph the damage for your insurer.
  4. Call dispatch and say what the building is, where the water is coming from, and whether the shutoff worked.

Commercial leak detection

A leak in a commercial building is rarely a simple drip under a sink. Water travels along a ceiling deck, down a chase or across a slab before it shows up, and by then the visible spot may be far from the source. Guessing wastes time and drywall.

We narrow it down in steps. First we read the water meter with every fixture off to confirm a leak exists. Then we pressure-test separate branches to learn which one is losing water. Acoustic listening gear and a camera can then pinpoint the pipe, so repair means a small opening instead of a demolition.

Signs a hidden leak is costing your business money

  • The meter keeps moving after every fixture is shut off
  • A musty smell in a closet, storeroom or back hallway
  • Ceiling tiles that stain, sag or fall without a roof leak
  • Paint that bubbles or flooring that lifts along one wall
  • A sudden jump in the water and sewer bill

Backflow testing for business properties

A backflow preventer is a valve assembly that stops water from running backward into the public supply. Restaurants, car washes, irrigation systems, fire sprinkler feeds and many multi-unit buildings have one. A failed assembly can let contaminated water reach the drinking supply, which is why testing matters.

Many water suppliers require testable assemblies to be inspected on a regular schedule. Ask your water utility or the Village of Romeoville which schedule and which paperwork apply to your property. We test the assembly, repair or replace worn parts, and give you the written result to file.

Buildings and equipment we work on

  • Retail units and strip centers, including shared restrooms
  • Offices and clinics with multiple fixture groups
  • Warehouses and light industrial space with floor drains and utility sinks
  • Restaurants and break rooms with grease-laden drains
  • Apartment buildings and rental properties managed by an owner or agent

Grease-heavy kitchen lines often need regular hydro jetting rather than a quick snake. Floor drains that back up in a warehouse may point to the main line, which is a job for sewer line cleaning.
