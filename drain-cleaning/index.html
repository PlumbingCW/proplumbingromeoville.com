A drain that will not clear becomes an emergency the moment water starts coming back up into a sink, tub or floor drain. Our emergency drain cleaning service in Romeoville, IL runs 24/7. We ask for one thing first: stop running water anywhere in the house. Every gallon you send down a blocked line has to go somewhere, and the lowest opening is usually a basement floor drain or a shower.

Backed-up drain right now: what to do while you wait

  1. Turn off the dishwasher and washing machine, and hold off on showers and flushing.
  2. Leave chemical drain cleaner on the shelf. Caustic liquid sits in a standing clog and makes the next visit riskier for whoever opens the trap.
  3. Move rugs and boxes away from the lowest floor drain.
  4. Tell dispatch whether one fixture or several are affected. Several slow fixtures point to the main line and may call for sewer line cleaning rather than a simple snake.

How we pick between a snake, a cable machine and a jetter

Not every clog needs the same tool. A hair-and-soap clog in a bathroom sink needs a hand auger and ten minutes. A kitchen line packed with years of grease needs a cable machine with a cutting head. A line that clogs every few weeks usually needs hydro jetting to scour the pipe wall instead of poking a hole through the middle of the blockage.

When one slow drain is really a main line problem

A single slow fixture is almost always a local clog. Two or three fixtures slowing down together, or a toilet that bubbles when the shower drains, means the shared line is restricted. Snaking the sink does nothing in that case. The right move is clearing the main, then a camera inspection if the problem comes back.

Signs the clog is deeper than one fixture

  • Water backs up in a tub or floor drain when you flush or run the washer
  • Several fixtures drain slowly at the same time
  • A gurgling sound from a toilet after the sink drains
  • Sewer smell near a basement floor drain

Kitchen sink clogs

Kitchen drains clog differently from bathroom drains. Cooking grease cools inside the pipe and coats the wall. It then catches rice, coffee grounds and pasta until the opening shrinks to a few fingers wide. A plunger moves the water around without fixing that.

We cable the line and flush it with hot water to carry the loosened grease out. Then we check whether the disposal or the dishwasher connection is feeding the problem.

What hard water does to Romeoville drains

Northern Illinois water is often hard, and hardness varies by water source and neighborhood, so ask the Village of Romeoville for its water quality report or test your own tap. Hard water leaves scale on pipe walls and binds with soap residue into the grey film we pull out of bathroom traps.

Scale will not clog a drain overnight. It narrows the pipe over the years and gives a small hair clog something to hold onto. A water softener reduces how much scale reaches your drains.

Codes and permits

Drain and sewer work in Illinois falls under the Illinois Plumbing Code, and the Village of Romeoville building department can tell you when a repair needs a permit. Simple clog clearing usually does not, but opening or replacing pipe often does.
