Water Heater Replacement Cost in Romeoville, IL: What Sets the Price

The price of a new water heater is the unit plus everything needed to install it safely. Tank type, size, venting, gas or electric connections and removal of the old heater all move the total.

We do not print a dollar figure here, because a number from one house tells you little about yours. This guide shows what builds the price so you can judge any quote.

By the Romeoville Pro Plumbing team · Published October 29, 2026

Replacing a water heater in Romeoville costs more or less depending on seven things. They are the heater type, its capacity, the fuel, the venting, the existing connections, local code work and the time of the call. A like-for-like swap of a standard tank is the simplest case. Changing fuel, moving the heater or upgrading to tankless adds work, and the price grows with it.

Tank or tankless: how the costs compare

Tankless costs more up front, and the savings come from continuous supply and a longer service life. A tank is cheaper to buy and easier to swap. The right choice depends on how long you plan to stay and how much hot water the household uses at once. Our tankless water heater page covers the sizing questions in detail.

The seven items on a typical quote

  1. The heater itself, matched to your household size and fuel.
  2. Labor to drain, disconnect and remove the old unit.
  3. New connectors, shutoff valve and a pressure relief valve.
  4. Venting for gas models, including any liner or new pipe the code requires.
  5. An expansion tank if your system calls for one.
  6. Permit and inspection, when the town requires them.
  7. Disposal of the old heater.

A quote that lists these items lets you compare one company with another. A quote that gives only a single number is harder to judge. Ask which of the seven are included and which are extra.

What pushes the price up

Cost factors for a water heater replacement

Capacity
A larger tank or a high-flow tankless unit costs more than a compact one.
Fuel change
Moving from electric to gas, or the reverse, means new lines or a new circuit.
Venting
A gas heater needs a vent that meets code, and an old or damaged flue adds work.
Location
A heater in a tight closet, attic or crawl space takes longer to swap than one in an open basement.
Condition of nearby pipes
Corroded shutoff valves or supply lines often need replacing at the same time.

Repair or replace: a quick decision list

Replace instead of repair when

  • The tank is leaking from the body, not from a fitting
  • The unit is well past the age most tanks last and the repair bill is rising
  • Rusty water comes from the hot tap but not the cold
  • Popping or rumbling noises come from mineral buildup at the bottom of the tank
  • The pilot or heating element fails repeatedly

A leak from the tank itself is not repairable, since the steel shell has corroded through. If the symptom is a tripped thermostat or a failed element, a water heater repair may be enough, and it is worth asking before you commit to a new unit.

How hard water changes the cost picture near Romeoville

Hardness differs between neighboring utilities. Lockport's 2024 water quality report states that the hardness of its water ranges from 36 to 42 grains per gallon. Plainfield's water service page states 6.3 grains per gallon. The Village of Romeoville publishes its own water quality information, so ask for it or test your tap.

The tie-back is to the heater. Minerals settle at the bottom of a tank and coat the elements of an electric unit and the heat exchanger in a tankless unit. Harder water usually means more flushing and a shorter life for the same equipment.

When you compare quotes, ask whether the installer recommends a flush schedule or a water softener for your tap water. That recurring upkeep belongs in the real cost of the heater.

Ways to keep the cost sensible

  • Pick a heater sized to the household, not the biggest one on the shelf
  • Keep the same fuel and location unless you have a reason to change
  • Replace worn shutoff valves and connectors at the same visit, since the heater is already drained
  • Ask about the age of the venting and have it checked while the old unit is out
  • Schedule the work during the day if the situation is not an emergency

Emergency timing matters. A failed heater on a winter night is the most costly time to buy, because you have less room to compare. If your tank is old, replacing it on your own timeline is the cheaper path.
