Emergency Garbage Disposal Repair in Romeoville, IL

A disposal that hums and will not spin, or leaks into the cabinet below, can shut down your kitchen. We repair and replace garbage disposals in Romeoville, IL, and our emergency line is open 24/7 for leaks and backups.

Garbage disposal trouble comes in three forms: it will not run, it runs but will not drain, or it leaks. Each has a different fix. We serve Romeoville 24/7, and for leaks and sink backups we dispatch immediately rather than waiting for a business-hours slot.

If your disposal is leaking under the sink right now

  1. Turn off the wall switch and unplug the unit if there is a plug under the sink. Never reach into a disposal that has power.
  2. Put a bowl under the drip and empty the cabinet of cleaners and boxes.
  3. Run no water in that sink until the leak is found. The dishwasher drains through the disposal and will also leak.
  4. Call dispatch and tell us where the water appears, at the top, the side or the bottom of the unit.

Where the water shows tells us the repair. A drip at the top is usually a cheap gasket. A drip from the underside of the canister means the unit has corroded through and must be replaced. That is the reason we ask before arriving.

Freeing a jam safely

Most disposals have a hex socket in the center of the underside, sized for an Allen key. Working it back and forth by hand frees a jammed flywheel. After that, press the red reset button on the bottom and run cold water briefly to test.

Never put your hand down the drain. If the key does not free it, a spoon, a bottle cap or a fork may be lodged inside, and we can retrieve it with the right tools. Repeated jams point to a disposal that is overloaded, undersized or nearing the end of its life.

Repair or replace

A disposal usually lasts several years, depending on use. Replacement beats repair when the housing leaks, the motor burns out or the unit rusts through. Repair works for a loose mount, a worn gasket or a simple jam.

  • Repair: gasket or mounting ring leaks, dishwasher hose connection, reset or jam issues
  • Replace: leak from the body, burned-out motor, rusted housing, grinding that does not clear
  • Upgrade: a larger motor or a batch-feed model when your household grinds a lot of food scraps

Installing a new disposal

Installation includes removing the old unit, cleaning the sink flange, setting a new mounting assembly, wiring or plugging in, reconnecting the dishwasher drain and testing for leaks. If your sink never had a disposal, we also check that there is a switch or a safe place to put an outlet.

Horsepower choices run from small motors suited to a couple of people to larger ones for big families. Stainless grinding parts last longer than plated ones. Tell us how many people cook in your kitchen, and we will help you choose.

Signs your disposal is near the end

  • Rust stains at the base or around the flange
  • Persistent odor even after cleaning
  • Repeated jams from ordinary scraps
  • A growl or rattle that was not there last year
  • Water seeping at the seam between the upper and lower housing

What not to put down it, and why it matters in Romeoville

Grease, coffee grounds, pasta, rice and potato peels build up in drain lines even when the disposal grinds them. Older kitchen drains in homes from the 1950s and 1960s, including many in Hampton Park, often have narrow or rough pipe that catches residue. Scrape plates into the trash and run cold water for several seconds after use.
